# Hornet on Gwen — Complete (v1.3.0)

Hornet from Hollow Knight: Silksong, cast on Gwen. The needle was always
Needlework — this makes it official.

**Inside the fantome:**
- Full Hornet model: white horned mask, short crimson cloak, slender
  backward-curving dancer legs, continuous integrated hip/rear/thigh
  shaping, custom animation set
- Needle replacing the scissors, with recoloured silk-white and crimson
  effects — no ghost-blue scissors survive
- Full voice pack: all 144 Gwen lines performed in Pharloom speak through
  a clone of Hornet's Silksong voice. Not English — she speaks her own
  language, and the barks are hers: Shaw! Hegale! Adino! Garama!
- Loading screen, profile circle and square, and the full ability icon
  set, all cut to exact stock sizes

**Install:** import the .fantome with your mod manager (LTK Manager /
cslol-manager) and enable it. The voice rides in the same package.

Hornet and Hollow Knight: Silksong belong to Team Cherry. Fan work,
made with love for the little plush knight pretending to be a bug.
