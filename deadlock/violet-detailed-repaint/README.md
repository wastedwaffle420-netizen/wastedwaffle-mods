The full 4096 repaint, finished:

- Every skin sheet repainted at 4096×4096 — face, torso, legs and arms now match the face/hair texel density.
- Extreme, uneven face melanation: deep, patchy, feathered pigmentation worked into the skin like real melanin — periorbital depth, lid creases, lip edges — never a flat mask.

The tail falls across her back again, so the spine work lives underneath the hair as an easter egg: you catch it in glimpses when the tail swings aside.
- The ponytail is Froggone's original, untouched — full length, tie and all, swinging on its own cloth exactly as it shipped.

### Install (v10)

The pak ships as split parts in this folder: `Violet-Detailed-Repaint-v10.zip.part01` through `.part05`.

1. Download all five parts into one folder.
2. Join them back into one zip. On Windows, open a command prompt in that folder and run:
   `copy /b Violet-Detailed-Repaint-v10.zip.part* Violet-Detailed-Repaint-v10.zip`
   On Mac/Linux: `cat Violet-Detailed-Repaint-v10.zip.part* > Violet-Detailed-Repaint-v10.zip`
3. Unzip it — inside is `violet-detailed-repaint-v10_dir.vpk`.
4. Drop the .vpk into your Deadlock addons folder (`game/citadel/addons`) or drag it into your mod manager. Remove any older Violet skin pak first — v10 replaces them completely.
5. Voice: download **Violet-Jasmine-Voice-v1.zip** from this folder, unzip it, and add the voice pak the same way. The manager will say the two paks conflict — they don't (one is model and skin, the other is sound). Enable both.
