# Concept art prompt — Dragon Lady Kench, the Shantytown Madam (paste into Chat with BOTH model-viewer shots attached)

Edit these two reference images of base Tahm Kench. This is a RETEXTURE concept:
the creature, pose, silhouette, body proportions, and every garment stay EXACTLY
as they are. Nothing is added, removed, resized, or remodeled. Only surface
color and painted detail change — everything below must read as paint on the
existing model, flat where the model is flat.

WHO SHE IS NOW: not a royal empress — a shantytown trash matriarch who runs the
brothels on the docks. The river's madam. Cheap glamour worn with total
confidence: too much makeup, too much jewelry, everything a little faded,
nothing about her apologetic. Big, unbothered, in charge of the whole pier.

PAINT CHANGES, region by region:

- SKIN: keep the green-to-cream base (dark river green on the head and back,
  pale cream belly), but warm it — she is flushed, lived-in. Rouge painted high
  on both cheeks. Deep red lipstick along the whole mouth seam so the grin
  reads made-up. Dark smoky eyeshadow around the glowing yellow eye. One small
  dark beauty mark beside the mouth. Slight sheen, like powder over river damp.
- VEST (the burgundy one with the two gold frog clasps): deepen to brothel red,
  worn shiny at the edges, with a faded floral brocade pattern woven in —
  peonies, dark on dark red. The two gold toggles stay, but tarnished and a
  little gaudy.
- COAT (the brown draped coat + tooled leather arm piece): darker, rattier —
  patched in two places with mismatched cloth, the gold trim gone dull. The
  tooled leather arm guard becomes stacked cheap bangles painted in bands of
  jade green and dull gold up the forearm.
- BELLY: bare and proud. Paint a string of cheap gold coins on a chain draped
  across it, flat against the skin. A faded wave-and-cloud tattoo low on one
  side of the belly, old ink, blue-grey. (No fish motifs anywhere.)
- HAT: the little black top hat stays, retextured as her good hat — the red
  band richer, the gold coin charm kept and polished brighter than anything
  else she owns, one painted red flower tucked at the band.
- CLAWS (hands and feet): painted lacquer red, like nail polish, a couple of
  them chipped.
- BARBELS AND TONGUE: the long whisker barbels stay pale flesh, cared-for,
  almost like jewelry themselves. The tongue stays its long pink self, maybe a
  touch deeper pink at the tip, as if recently licked over lipstick.
- TEETH AND EYE: unchanged — the grin and the one glowing eye are already hers.

MOOD OF THE IMAGE: model-viewer neutral, same grey backdrop, same two angles
(front and three-quarter) as the references. This is a texture reference sheet,
not a splash art: no scenery, no props, no extra characters, no new geometry —
no hair, no fan, no cigarette, nothing she isn't already wearing. If a detail
can't be painted onto the existing surface, leave it out.

Negative direction: no empress, no crown, no imperial gold-and-jade palace
look, no clean beauty, no young face, no slimmer body, no fish or koi motifs,
no snakes, no new clothing items, no geometry changes.
