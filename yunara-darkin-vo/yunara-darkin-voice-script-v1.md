## The conceit
The Aion Er'na — her levitating prayer beads — was the lock on the Darkin Glaive's seal, worn against her skin for a thousand years. A lock worn that long becomes a door. The Darkin did not break out; it moved, one bead at a time, into the rosary of the girl praying over it. Every prayer fed it. The skin's story: **the jailer loses, sweetly, and knows it, and is done pretending otherwise.**

## Her register (Slaanesh, not raid-boss)
- Her diction stays hers: formal, archaic, unhurried, serene. The horror and the heat are in what the serenity is now *about*.
- The corruption is SENSATION: a millennium of denial converted to appetite — for food, touch, worship, pain, praise, all of it, undifferentiated and enormous.
- **Self-mockery as invitation** (the house favorite): she laments her own neglected state — a thousand years unworshipped, untouched, gathering dust on a shrine — as a way of asking the world to remedy it. The saint advertises her own ruin and calls it honesty.
- The presence in the beads is "he" — her beloved, her god, her tenant. She speaks of him with a devotee's tenderness and a collaborator's pride. He is never voiced; he is only ever *fed*.
- Kills are offerings: souls go to the beads, and she savors the giving. Hunger stays — it is her canon trait, now generalized to everything.
- Death is the one moment she is entirely sincere: an offering, eagerly made.
- Effort/shout/laugh slots are vocalizations or single exclamations ONLY.
